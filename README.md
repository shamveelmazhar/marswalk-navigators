# Marswalk Navigators

A route planner for the first astronauts to walk on Mars.

Our team is building this for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge, "Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map". The idea is simple. Before a crew steps outside on Mars, they need to know which way to walk, what the ground is like, how much radiation they'll pick up, and whether their support rover can follow them. We pull real NASA data together into one map that answers those questions.

We're starting at Mount Sharp in Gale Crater, where the Curiosity rover studied the "boxwork" ridges in 2025.

## What we're working on

- **Robotics:** a path planner that finds safe routes for an astronaut and a support rover over real terrain, avoiding steep slopes and rough ground.
- **Aerospace:** planning a Marswalk around its time, energy and radiation budgets, using Curiosity's own radiation measurements.
- **FEA:** turning the worst terrain along a route into load cases, and checking a rover wheel or suspension part against them.

More details will follow as the project comes together over the hackathon weekend.
